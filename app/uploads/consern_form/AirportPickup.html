AIRPORT PICKUP & ARRIVAL PROTOCOL BRIEF
IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS: Please review this arrival protocol carefully before your departure. This document outlines your coordinated airport reception, safety parameters, communication guidelines, and logistics management upon arrival at your medical destination.
1. DESIGNATED RECEPTION & MEETING POINTS

Upon clearing customs, immigration, and baggage claim areas, you are directed to proceed straight to the main international arrival exit terminal gate. A dedicated MEDITRAVEL logistics representative or professional chauffeur will be waiting for you at the designated greeting zone. Our representative will be prominently holding a digital or physical placard displaying the MEDITRAVEL corporate logo alongside your primary surname.

In the event of severe flight delays, early arrivals, or unexpected bottlenecks within baggage claim, do not exit the primary terminal building complex alone. Please remain within the secure, well-monitored greeting area and refer immediately to the emergency contact protocol outlined below.

2. MANDATORY COMMUNICATION & CONNECTIVITY PROTOCOL

It is critical that your mobile device remains fully operational and connected immediately upon landing. You are required to enable international roaming data or instantly connect to the airport's complimentary public wireless internet network. Upon successful disembarkation, please send a brief arrival confirmation message via SMS, WhatsApp, or email directly to your assigned MEDITRAVEL personal care coordinator.

3. IMMEDIATE LOGISTICS & EMERGENCY CONTACTS
Primary Care Coordinator (Available via voice & WhatsApp)
24/7 Urgent Logistics Hotline (Dedicated for immediate transport/routing anomalies)
Corporate Support Desk (For non-urgent updates & document submissions)
4. SECURE GROUND TRANSPORTATION & LUGGAGE HANDLING

All ground transfers managed by MEDITRAVEL utilize exclusively vetted, fully licensed, and commercially insured transportation vehicles tailored to your specific clinical comfort and mobility requirements. Your representative will safely assist you with all heavy luggage and provide secure transit directly from the airport terminal to your pre-arranged hotel accommodation or designated clinical check-in facility.

For your health and physical safety—especially when traveling prior to or directly following complex clinical interventions—you are strictly instructed to refrain from lifting heavy bags or executing strenuous physical movements during the transfer window. Allow our transport staff to manage all manual luggage handling.
